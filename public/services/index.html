Our Services

Full-lifecycle real estate development services. From land acquisition and entitlements through project financing, construction oversight, and asset management, we develop opportunities from the ground up.

Service 01

Land Acquisition & Site Selection

Strategic site identification and land acquisition for multi-family, commercial, and mixed-use development. We analyze markets, evaluate parcels, and negotiate purchases to position your project for success from day one.

  • Market analysis and demographic research for target areas
  • Site evaluation including environmental, geotechnical, and utility assessments
  • Purchase contract negotiation and due diligence management
  • Zoning and land use compatibility analysis
  • Highest-and-best-use studies to maximize development potential

Service 02

Entitlements & Permitting

Navigating the regulatory landscape from rezoning through building permits. We manage the entitlement process to move projects from concept to shovel-ready, working with municipalities, planning commissions, and community stakeholders.

  • Zoning applications, variances, and conditional use permits
  • Site plan review and subdivision plat approvals
  • Environmental impact assessments and compliance
  • Community engagement and public hearing representation
  • Building permit coordination and expediting

Service 03

Project Financing & Capital Stack

Structuring the right capital stack for each project. We work with lenders, equity partners, and investors to secure construction and permanent financing that aligns with your development strategy and return objectives.

  • Debt and equity structuring for development projects
  • Construction loan sourcing and lender relationship management
  • Tax credit and incentive program identification (LIHTC, Opportunity Zones, TIF)
  • Pro forma development and financial modeling
  • Draw management and lender reporting throughout construction

Service 04

Owner's Representation

Your dedicated advocate on every development decision. We act as the owner's agent, protecting your interests in dealings with architects, contractors, municipalities, and lenders throughout the development process.

  • Independent oversight of contractor and design team performance
  • Pay application review and draw management
  • Change order negotiation and scope protection
  • Schedule monitoring with early warning indicators
  • Regular owner reporting with financial and schedule dashboards

Service 05

Pre-Development Services

Critical planning and analysis before capital commitment. We conduct feasibility studies, develop pro formas, select design teams, and create project plans that reduce risk and set the stage for successful execution.

  • Feasibility analysis and highest-and-best-use studies
  • Development pro forma creation and sensitivity analysis
  • Architect, engineer, and consultant team selection
  • Conceptual design and programming oversight
  • Development timeline and milestone planning

Service 06

Asset Management

Post-completion property oversight and performance optimization. We manage developed assets to maximize returns, implementing value-add strategies and maintaining the quality standards established during construction.

  • Property performance monitoring and financial reporting
  • Value-add strategy development and execution
  • Capital improvement planning and budgeting
  • Property management oversight and vendor coordination
  • Disposition strategy and exit planning
