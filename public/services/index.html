Construction management (CM) provides oversight, coordination, and advisory services on behalf of the owner, while general contracting (GC) means we hold the prime contract and self-perform or subcontract all work. US Development offers both, and our integrated family of companies allows us to self-perform critical trades like framing, drywall, and exteriors under either model.

By controlling framing, drywall, and exterior trades under one management structure, we eliminate coordination gaps that typically cost 50-100 hours per project.
