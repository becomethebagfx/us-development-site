The US Construction Companies: Framing Through Finish

January 5, 2026 US Development Team 14 min read

What started as a single framing company in Kentucky in the early 1990s has grown into one of the most comprehensive integrated construction platforms in the United States. The US Construction Companies now encompasses five distinct specialty contractors that together cover the full scope of commercial construction from structural framing through interior finish. Each company operates as a specialist in its trade, but the real power of the model lies in how these companies work together under unified management, shared technology, and coordinated field operations. For general contractors and developers building multi-family, hospitality, senior living, and mixed-use projects, the US Family offers something that no single subcontractor and no ad-hoc team of independent subs can match: true integration from framing through finish, managed by a single management team, delivered under a single contract, and backed by a single warranty.

The Origin Story: US Framing International

The US Construction Companies traces its origins to the early 1990s, when Thomas English founded US Framing in Louisville, Kentucky. The company began as a regional wood framing contractor serving the multi-family residential market in the Southeast. From the beginning, the company's approach was distinctive. Rather than treating wood framing as a commodity trade where the lowest bid wins, English built US Framing around a production framing model that emphasized efficiency, quality consistency, and the ability to scale capacity to meet the demands of large, repetitive multi-family projects.

That production-oriented approach proved to be a decisive competitive advantage. While many framing contractors struggled to maintain quality and schedule consistency on projects above 100 units, US Framing's systems-based approach allowed the company to deliver reliable performance on projects of 200, 300, and even 500 or more units. General contractors and developers discovered that working with US Framing meant one less variable to worry about on their projects. The framing would be on schedule, it would meet quality standards, and the crew would show up every day. In an industry plagued by unreliable subcontractors, this consistency was worth its weight in gold.

Over three decades, US Framing International has grown into the nation's largest multi-family wood framing group, delivering more than 15 million square feet of commercial framing per year across the United States. The company's portfolio includes some of the largest and most complex wood-framed multi-family projects in the country. But the growth of US Framing was just the beginning of the US Family story. As the company's relationships with general contractors and developers deepened, a pattern emerged: the same clients who relied on US Framing for consistent, high-quality structural framing were struggling with the same coordination challenges when it came to the trades that followed framing on the critical path. That observation planted the seed for what would become the US Construction family of companies.

Quick Answer

The US Construction Companies is an integrated group of specialty construction contractors including US Framing International, US Drywall, US Exteriors, US Development, and US Interiors. Founded in the early 1990s by Thomas English, the family provides integrated multi-trade construction services for commercial and multi-family projects nationwide.

US Framing International: The Nation's Largest Multi-Family Wood Framing Group

US Framing International remains the cornerstone of the US Family. As the nation's largest multi-family wood framing group, the company delivers production-scale structural wood framing for commercial buildings up to five stories. The company's capabilities include conventional stick framing, panelized wall systems, engineered floor and roof systems, and pre-fabricated components. With the capacity to deliver more than 15 million square feet of commercial framing annually, US Framing has the scale to take on the largest multi-family and mixed-use projects in the country while maintaining the quality standards and schedule reliability that built the company's reputation.

US Framing's pre-construction services are a critical differentiator. The company's engineering and estimating team works with architects and developers during the design phase to optimize structural systems for cost and constructability. Value engineering analysis identifies opportunities to reduce material usage, simplify connections, and streamline the construction sequence without compromising structural performance. BIM coordination ensures that the framing model aligns with MEP systems before the first stud is delivered to the site. This pre-construction investment pays dividends during construction, reducing RFIs, minimizing field changes, and ensuring that the building can be framed efficiently and accurately.

US Drywall: Commercial Drywall, Acoustical Ceilings, and Specialty Finishes

US Drywall was established to bring the same production-oriented, quality-focused approach that built US Framing to the commercial drywall trade. The company provides full-scope commercial drywall services including metal framing for interior partitions, gypsum board installation, taping and finishing, acoustical ceiling systems, and specialty wall and ceiling finishes. US Drywall serves the same multi-family, hospitality, senior living, and mixed-use markets as its sister companies, and the company's crews are experienced in the specific requirements of these building types, from fire-rated demising walls in multi-family buildings to sound-rated assemblies in hospitality applications.

The integration between US Framing and US Drywall delivers immediate, measurable benefits. Because both companies operate under the same management structure and use the same BIM coordination platform, the handoff from structural framing to drywall installation is seamless. The framing is built to drywall-ready standards, with backing, blocking, and fire-stopping installed during the framing phase rather than requiring a separate mobilization for corrections. Dimensional tolerances are coordinated so that the drywall installation proceeds without the shimming, furring, and field adjustments that are common when the framing and drywall trades are performed by independent subcontractors with different quality standards.

US Exteriors: Siding, Roofing, EIFS, Waterproofing, and Exterior Trim

US Exteriors completes the exterior envelope, providing comprehensive exterior cladding and waterproofing services. The company's scope includes fiber cement and vinyl siding installation, commercial roofing systems, EIFS (Exterior Insulation and Finish Systems), waterproofing and weather barrier installation, exterior trim and fascia, soffit systems, and rain screen assemblies. US Exteriors brings the same systematic, production-oriented approach to exterior work that characterizes the entire US Family, with crews trained specifically in the multi-family and commercial building types that the family serves.

The coordination between US Framing and US Exteriors is particularly critical because the structural framing directly supports and interfaces with the exterior cladding system. When these two trades are performed by independent subcontractors, the exterior cladding installer frequently discovers that framing members are not positioned correctly for cladding attachment, that blocking for heavy cladding elements was not installed during framing, or that the weather barrier substrate is not within the tolerances required by the cladding manufacturer. Each of these discoveries triggers a change order, a correction cycle, and a schedule delay. Under the US Family model, exterior cladding requirements are incorporated into the framing plan from the beginning, and the framing is executed with full awareness of what the exterior trade needs. Blocking, backing, and attachment points for the cladding system are installed during the framing phase, not retrofitted afterward.

US Development: The Management Layer

US Development is the management and coordination layer that ties the US Family together. While each sister company excels as a specialist in its trade, US Development provides the unified project management, scheduling, quality control, and client interface that transform four independent specialty contractors into a single, integrated project delivery team. US Development is where the integrated project delivery model lives, serving as the single point of contact for general contractors and developers who want to access the full capabilities of the US Construction family through one contract, one schedule, and one warranty.

Quick Answer

The US Family integrated model works by placing framing, drywall, exteriors, and interiors under one management structure coordinated by US Development. All companies share BIM models, scheduling systems, safety protocols, and quality standards. One phone call to US Development activates multi-trade coordination with a single contract, schedule, and warranty.

US Development's pre-construction team engages early in the project lifecycle, working with the GC and design team to develop integrated scoping, scheduling, and pricing for all trades in the US Family package. During construction, US Development's project managers and superintendents oversee all family trades on the project, conducting daily coordination meetings, managing the master schedule, performing cross-trade quality inspections, and serving as the GC's single point of contact for everything from RFI responses to schedule updates to change order pricing. This coordination function is not an add-on or an overhead cost; it is the mechanism that generates the schedule compression, cost savings, and quality improvements that make the turnkey model financially superior to the traditional multi-sub approach.

US Development also provides general contracting and owner's representative services for clients who want US Development to serve as the primary contractor rather than as a multi-trade subcontractor to another GC. In this capacity, US Development manages not only the US Construction group's trades but also the full scope of additional subcontractors required to complete the project, bringing the same coordination discipline and systems-based approach to the entire construction process.

US Interiors: Commercial Interior Finishing

The newest member of the US Construction family, US Interiors extends the group's integrated capabilities into the interior finish trades. US Interiors provides acoustical ceilings, specialty finishes, painting and coatings, millwork and trim, wall coverings, and interior restoration. With US Interiors, the US Construction group now covers the entire critical path from structural framing through final interior finish, delivering a truly comprehensive package for every major trade involved in constructing and finishing a multi-family or commercial building.

The strategic rationale for US Interiors follows the same logic that drove the creation of each previous family company. Interior finish trades have significant interface dependencies with the drywall and framing trades that precede them. The quality and dimensional accuracy of the drywall installation directly affects the quality achievable by the painting contractor. The levelness of the subfloor, which is determined by the framing, directly affects the flooring installation. By bringing these finish trades into the US Family, the same cross-trade coordination, shared quality standards, and unified scheduling that has proven so effective for framing, drywall, and exteriors will be extended to cover the final phases of interior construction.

The Integrated Advantage: One Call Starts Multi-Trade Coordination

The US Family model delivers a deceptively simple value proposition: one phone call to US Development activates a fully integrated multi-trade construction team. Behind that simplicity lies a sophisticated operational infrastructure that has been refined over three decades of multi-family and commercial construction. Shared BIM models ensure that every trade works from the same digital truth. Unified scheduling eliminates the gaps and cascading delays that plague the traditional multi-sub model. Cross-trade quality inspections catch defects at the source rather than letting them propagate through subsequent trades. Integrated safety programs reduce the complexity and cost of maintaining consistent safety standards across multiple trades.

For the general contractor, the practical benefits are immediate and measurable. One contract instead of three or four. One set of insurance certificates instead of three or four. One schedule to monitor instead of three or four independent schedules that must be coordinated. One quality control program to review instead of three or four. One point of contact for all multi-trade coordination issues. And one warranty that covers the entire integrated scope, eliminating the finger-pointing and warranty disputes that consume management time and erode trust in the traditional model.

Real-World Impact: Schedule Compression, Cost Savings, and Single Warranty

The impact of the US Family's integrated model is not theoretical. Across hundreds of projects, the model consistently delivers quantifiable improvements in the metrics that matter most to general contractors and developers. Schedule compression of two to six weeks is typical, depending on project size and the number of integrated trades. Cost savings of $150,000 to $400,000 per project are common on mid-size to large multi-family developments. Drywall rework rates fall below 2 percent compared to the 5 to 8 percent industry average. Change orders at trade interfaces are virtually eliminated. And the GC's project management team recovers 30 to 50 percent of the time they would otherwise spend on inter-trade coordination.

Quick Answer

The US Construction Companies includes five entities: US Framing International (structural wood framing, 15M+ sqft/year), US Drywall (commercial drywall and acoustical ceilings), US Exteriors (siding, roofing, EIFS, waterproofing), US Development (project management layer), and US Interiors (coming soon -- flooring, painting, millwork, specialty finishes).

The single warranty advantage deserves particular emphasis because it addresses one of the most persistent pain points in commercial construction. When a building owner reports a wall performance issue two years after occupancy, the investigation under the traditional model becomes a forensic exercise in blame allocation. Was it the framing? The insulation? The drywall? The exterior cladding? The waterproofing? Each subcontractor has a strong incentive to point the finger at another trade, and the building owner is left waiting months for a resolution. Under the US Family model, wall performance is a system-level warranty backed by US Development. If the wall is not performing, US Development diagnoses the issue, determines which trade or combination of trades is responsible, and executes the repair. The building owner makes one call and gets one resolution, without the multi-party dispute that the traditional model almost inevitably produces.

The financial impact of this warranty structure extends beyond the cost of individual repairs. Developers and property managers who have experienced the frustration of multi-party warranty disputes assign real value to the simplicity and reliability of a single-source warranty. For repeat clients, the US Family's warranty model is often cited as one of the primary reasons they continue to choose integrated delivery over the traditional multi-sub approach. The reduction in warranty management overhead for the building owner can save tens of thousands of dollars over the first five years of building operation.

Future Vision: Covering Every Interior and Exterior Trade

The US Construction Companies is not finished growing. The strategic vision is to build an integrated platform that covers every major trade involved in the construction and finishing of commercial and multi-family buildings. The launch of US Interiors represents the next step in this expansion, extending the family's integrated capabilities into the interior finish trades that complete the building for occupancy. Beyond US Interiors, the family is evaluating opportunities to add additional specialty trades that have significant coordination dependencies with the existing family companies.

The expansion strategy follows a deliberate pattern. Each new company added to the family must meet two criteria: the trade must have significant interface dependencies with existing family trades, meaning that integration delivers measurable coordination value; and the trade must be large enough in scope and cost to justify the investment in building a full-service specialty contractor. Trades that meet both criteria represent opportunities to extend the family's integrated advantage and deliver even greater value to clients. Trades that do not meet both criteria are better served by independent subcontractors managed through conventional coordination methods.

The long-term vision is a construction platform where a general contractor or developer can engage a single entity for every trade from foundation through finish. While that vision is still evolving, the current US Family already covers the majority of the critical path for wood-framed commercial buildings. Structural framing, interior drywall, exterior cladding, and soon interior finishes represent approximately 50 to 60 percent of the above-grade construction scope on a typical multi-family project. That level of integration, managed through US Development's coordination platform, already delivers transformative results for the clients who choose the US Family model.
