About US Development

The real estate development arm of US Construction, delivering full-lifecycle development services for owners, investors, and developers nationwide.

Our Story

Built to Develop. Backed by Construction Expertise.

US Development was founded in 2020 as the real estate development arm of what is now US Construction. Born from the success of US Framing -- established in the early 1990s by Thomas English -- US Development was created to bring deep construction expertise to every phase of real estate development, from land acquisition and entitlements through project financing and asset management.

As the US Construction family grew to include US Framing, US Drywall, US Exteriors, and the forthcoming US Interiors, US Development evolved into the full-lifecycle development company that leverages the construction capabilities of its sister companies. Our development team understands what it takes to build because our family does it every day.

Headquartered at 4965 US Highway 42, Suite 220, Louisville, Kentucky, US Development serves as the real estate development platform of US Construction, identifying opportunities and managing projects from concept through completion and beyond.

Our Mission

"To create value through real estate development by combining deep construction expertise with strategic site selection, smart financing, and disciplined project execution."

Our Journey

The US Family Timeline

From a single framing crew to a fully integrated family of construction companies.

1990s

US Framing Founded

Thomas English establishes US Framing International with a small crew in Kentucky. The foundation of what would become a family of construction companies begins with a commitment to quality craftsmanship and safety.

2010s

US Drywall Launched

Recognizing the natural synergy between framing and drywall, the US Family expands with the launch of US Drywall, offering interior finishing capabilities to complement structural framing.

2018

US Exteriors Launched

US Exteriors joins the family, adding siding, EIFS, stucco, and building envelope capabilities. The vision of a framing-through-finish operation begins to take shape.

2020

US Development Created

US Development is established as the real estate development arm of the family. Bringing construction expertise to land acquisition, entitlements, financing, and full-lifecycle project development.

2024

US Interiors Announced

The US Family announces US Interiors, expanding the integrated service offering to include interior finishing and build-outs, completing the framing-through-finish promise.

2026

US Construction Family Serving Nationwide

The US Construction family now serves markets nationwide with integrated development and construction services -- US Development leading real estate development while sister companies deliver construction trades.
